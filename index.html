Term 3 throwback

Did anything go wrong?

do (someone's) head in
Really annoy or frustrate someone
dodgy
Not reliable, not honest, or not quite right
not on
Not acceptable, not fair
my bad
My mistake. Sorry, that was my fault.
it's a lot

sort (something) out
Fix or organise a problem

What's next for you?

tossing up
Trying to decide between two choices
up in the air
Not decided yet, uncertain
locked in
Confirmed and decided. It will not change now.
